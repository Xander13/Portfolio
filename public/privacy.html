Privacy Policy


Effective Date: Sept. 1st, 2026





1. Overview


Welcome to the personal portfolio of Alex Kauffman. Your privacy is straightforward here: this site does not collect, track, or sell your personal data.





2. Interactive Assistant ("Echo")


Echo is an assistant-like interaction that calls public APIs from selected parts of the site. It does not store personal data or sensitive information. It echoes information users want to access, including search, weather, stock quotes, and other live lookups.


Echo is not an artificial intelligence model, automated bot, or machine learning agent. It is a front-end interface that processes structured JSON queries to simulate interactive responses locally in your browser. No messages, prompts, inputs, or outputs are collected, transmitted, or stored on any server.





3. Weather Feature & Location Data


The weather tool on this site requests permission to access your browser’s local geolocation data only if you explicitly select "YES" when prompted.


User Consent:

Geolocation access is entirely voluntary. If you reject or decline the prompt, no location data is requested or processed.



No Data Retention:

When permitted, your location coordinates are used instantly to fetch current weather conditions for your local session. This data is processed in real time and is never saved, logged, or stored.





4. Third-Party Data Sources


Echo includes optional features (such as web search, stock quotes, and weather lookups) that send your query directly from your browser to public third-party APIs to retrieve live information.


Client-Side Only:

These requests happen directly between your browser and the relevant third-party service. This site's servers never see, process, or relay them.



No Data Retention:

None of these queries or their results are logged, stored, or saved in any database associated with this website.





5. Local Storage (No Cookies)


This site does not use tracking cookies or advertising identifiers. It uses your browser's local storage only to remember your custom stock watchlist symbols between visits, entirely on your device. This data is never transmitted to or stored on any server.





6. Search Engine Indexing


This website allows standard search engine crawlers (such as Googlebot) to index public pages for search visibility using standard metadata tags:


○   <meta name="robots" content="index,follow">

○   <meta name="googlebot" content="index,follow">


These tags permit search engine crawlers to read public site content. They do not collect or track user interaction data.





7. External Links


This site links to external destinations, including LinkedIn, employer and project websites, and a downloadable resume PDF. Alex Kauffman does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these third-party sites.





8. Disclaimer of Warranties


Stock, weather, and search information displayed through Echo is provided "as is" for informational and demonstrative purposes only. It may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate, and should not be relied upon for financial, meteorological, or other decision-making purposes.
