About



Alex Kauffman is a designer focused on clarity, interaction, and systems-thinking. With a BFA in New Media Design from RIT, he blends creative direction and product thinking to design tools that support thoughtful, long-term growth.


He’s currently exploring roles in product design and visual systems — aiming to contribute to meaningful products, not just fast-moving ideas. His dream role is helping Microsoft’s Accessibility team improve the Xbox gaming experience for diverse players — or working with platforms like Toast to make everyday payments more accessible for all.


Outside of design, Alex is getting into motocross (MX) as a new hobby. He also enjoys traveling to different countries, meeting new people, and trying unfamiliar things — always chasing experiences that expand perspective.
