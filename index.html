About



Alex Kauffman is a design focused on systems-driven, data-driven design. With a BFA in New Media Design from RIT, he blends creative direction, product thinking, and technical execution to solve real business challenges through thoughtful solutions.


Rooted in computing and visual systems, Alex works across design, tooling, and early-stage product development. He’s currently exploring roles in product design, visual systems, or software prototyping — aiming to help shape innovative MVPs and lead R&D efforts at the edge of design and technology.


Long-term, I’m interested in shaping new creative disciplines that help teams align design with deeper values — exploring how ethos can be a driver in product, culture, and systems.
